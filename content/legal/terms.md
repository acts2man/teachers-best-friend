## 6. What the AI can and cannot do

The Service uses artificial intelligence to read uploaded work, propose scores,
suggest which standard a question measures, and recommend reteaching. It is useful and
it is also wrong sometimes, particularly with difficult handwriting, unusual layouts,
diagrams, and open-ended writing.

Because of that:

- Every AI result is presented as a **suggestion for your review**. It is labelled
  that way in the interface.
- **You confirm or correct results before they count.** Scores and standards
  recommendations are not recorded as final until you approve them.
- **Do not use the Service as the sole basis for a high-stakes decision** about a
  student, including grades of record, retention, placement, special education
  eligibility, or discipline. Use your own professional judgement, and other evidence.
- The Service does not diagnose disabilities or learning styles, and must not be used
  as though it does.

You remain the educator of record. The Service supports your judgement; it does not
replace it.
